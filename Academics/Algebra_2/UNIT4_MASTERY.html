📐 Unit 4 — Polynomial Functions

Degree & end behavior, factoring, division, theorems, zeros & multiplicity. Pass the test to unlock Unit 5.
Degree = highest exponent · leading coefficient = coeff of that term.
End behavior: even degree → both ends same way (up if LC>0, down if LC<0); odd degree → ends opposite (LC>0: down-left/up-right).
Remainder Theorem: remainder of f(x) ÷ (x − a) is f(a). If f(a)=0, (x−a) is a factor.
Multiplicity: (x−a)ⁿ → zero a with mult n. Even → graph touches; odd → crosses.
Sum/diff of cubes: a³±b³ = (a ± b)(a² ∓ ab + b²). Fundamental Thm: degree n → n roots (with complex & multiplicity).
