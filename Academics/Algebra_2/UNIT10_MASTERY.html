📐 Unit 10 — Probability

Odds, permutations & combinations, independent/dependent, and conditional. Pass to finish the course.
Probability: P(event) = favorable / total. Complement: P(not A) = 1 − P(A).
Permutation (order matters): ₙPᵣ = n!/(n−r)!. Combination (order doesn't): ₙCᵣ = n!/(r!(n−r)!).
Independent: P(A and B) = P(A)·P(B). Dependent: use P(B given A).
Mutually exclusive: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B). Conditional: P(B|A) = P(A and B)/P(A).
