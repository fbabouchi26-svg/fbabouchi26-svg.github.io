## HOW EACH CHAPTER WORKS (WEEKLY CYCLE)

### Day 1 (Monday/Tuesday): LEARN
- **Watch video lesson** for the first section of the chapter
- **Read the section** on the online textbook (2-3 pages)
- **Take notes** — write the key formulas, definitions, and one worked example
- **Do the Exploration** activity at the start of each section

### Day 2 (Tuesday/Wednesday): PRACTICE
- **Do the section exercises** (odd numbers first — answers available on BigIdeasMath.com)
- **Use the 4-Step Problem Solving template** for word problems
- **Check answers immediately** — redo any wrong ones

### Day 3 (Wednesday/Thursday): LEARN + PRACTICE
- **Watch video** for the second section of the chapter
- **Read + notes + exercises** — same process as Days 1-2
- **Flashcard review** — use the built-in Flash Cards from the textbook site

### Day 4 (Thursday/Friday): DEEP PRACTICE
- **Mixed practice** — exercises from BOTH sections
- **Play a Game Closet game** related to the chapter topic (10-15 min)
- **Use Dynamic Solutions Tool** to check understanding of worked examples

### Day 5 (Friday/Saturday): TEST
- **Take the Chapter Quiz** (mid-chapter quiz on the textbook site)
- **Do the Chapter Review** — cumulative problems
- **Take the Chapter Test** — time it. Grade honestly.

### Day 6 (Saturday/Sunday): FIX + EXTEND
- **Error correction** — redo every problem you got wrong on the test
- **Performance Task** — the real-world application problem at the end of each chapter
- **Cumulative Assessment** — connects to previous chapters
- **If solid:** Preview next chapter. If shaky: redo weak sections.
