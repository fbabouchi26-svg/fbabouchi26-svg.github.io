📐 Unit 8 — Compositions & Inverses

Function operations, compositions, and inverses. Pass the test to unlock Unit 9.
Operations: (f+g)(x)=f(x)+g(x) · (f·g)(x)=f(x)·g(x).
Composition f(g(x)): plug g INTO f. Work inside-out: f(g(2)) → do g(2) first, then f of that.
Find an inverse: swap x and y, then solve for y. (Inverse of mx+b is (x−b)/m.)
Inverses check: f and g are inverses if f(g(x)) = x AND g(f(x)) = x.
Graphically: inverses are reflections over the line y = x. x² needs a domain restriction to have an inverse.
