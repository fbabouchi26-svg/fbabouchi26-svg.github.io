📐 Unit 6 — Rational Exponents & Radical Functions

Fractional exponents, simplifying & operating with radicals, rationalizing, and solving. Pass the test to unlock Unit 7.
Rational exponents: x^(1/n) = ⁿ√x · x^(m/n) = (ⁿ√x)ᵐ. So 8^(2/3) = (∛8)² = 2² = 4.
Negative exponent: flip it: x^(−n) = 1/xⁿ.
Simplify √: pull out perfect squares (√72 = √(36·2) = 6√2). Combine only LIKE radicals.
Rationalize: multiply top & bottom by the radical (or the conjugate).
Solve: isolate the radical → raise both sides to the power → check for extraneous answers.
(Type roots as “sqrt”, e.g. 6sqrt2 for 6√2.)
