# Algebra 2 H — 21-Day Sprint (Mr. Rice, FHS)

**Goal:** Finish all 10 units + review in **3 weeks** (buffer to 4).
**Format:** **30-minute sessions, up to 4 a day (max 2 hrs/day).** Spread them out — he doesn't have to do them back-to-back.
**The rule:** Each unit ends with a **mastery test — pass 13/15 (87%) to unlock the next unit.** Fail it → review the misses, retake. No breezing through.

Every session = *watch/read the lesson → work problems on paper → check them.* The site is his textbook, in his format.

## Pace math
~72 half-hour sessions ÷ 4/day = **18 days of work**, leaving Days 19–21 for review — inside 3 weeks. If a hard unit (Polynomials, Rationals, Trig) needs an extra 2 sessions, it eats into the review buffer; that's the built-in **"week 4 if needed"** cushion. Skip/short a day → just push the finish a day or two.
