Ways to solve ax²+bx+c=0: factor (zero-product) · square roots (if no bx) · completing the square · quadratic formula x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a
Discriminant b²−4ac: >0 → 2 real · =0 → 1 real · <0 → 2 complex
Complete the square: for x²+bx, add (b/2)²
Complex numbers: i = √(−1), so i² = −1. Form a + bi. Combine like terms.
