📐 Unit 9 — Trigonometry

Right triangles, radians, reference angles, the unit circle, and graphing. Pass the test to unlock Unit 10.
SOH-CAH-TOA: sin = opp/hyp · cos = adj/hyp · tan = opp/adj.
Degrees ↔ radians: ×π/180 to go to radians; ×180/π to go to degrees. (180° = π)
Coterminal: add/subtract 360°. Reference angle (to the x-axis): Q2 → 180−θ · Q3 → θ−180 · Q4 → 360−θ.
Special values: sin30=1/2, cos30=√3/2 · sin45=cos45=√2/2, tan45=1 · sin60=√3/2, cos60=1/2.
Graphs of y = a·sin(bx)+d: amplitude = |a| · period = 2π/b · midline = y = d.
(Type radians like pi/6 and roots like sqrt3/2.)
