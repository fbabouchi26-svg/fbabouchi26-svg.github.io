# Ibrahim — Planning Rules (Claude's working memory)

Standing rules I apply when building day sheets. Fatima sets the week; these are the defaults.

## The athlete
- Ibrahim, 15. Stress-fracture history last year from overtraining → **load management is the #1 rule.**

## Load rules (injury prevention)
- **One impact activity per day, max.** Never two.
- **Impact** = field/soccer, basketball, running, jumping. **Low-impact** = swimming (zero), biking, fascia/mobility, grounding. **No load** = academics, reading, deen.
- Low-impact (swim/bike) can sit alongside an impact session as recovery; impact never stacks on impact.
- Strength = **fascia-driven resistance only** (bands, bodyweight, slow eccentrics, ISO holds). **No heavy lifting.** Keep leg-heavy work OFF impact days.
- Daily end-of-day check-in (what he did + pain: none/niggle/pain, site, trigger). Pain → next day low/rest. Recurring shin/foot pain → professional.

## Daily rhythm
- Up at **sunrise**, **grounding first** (he loves it). Bed by **10:30**.
- Heat rule: outdoor work **before 11am or after 7pm**.
- **Mon–Thu: soccer/basketball specific training is 9:30–10:45** (Fatima's preferred window).

## Default weekly shape (set fresh each Sunday around weather/appointments/matches)
- Field: Mon / Wed / Sat · Basketball: Tue / Thu
- Swim: Mon / Wed / Fri evening (never Tuesday) · Bike: Fri + easy Sun
- Sunday = rest/reset + weekly planning
- Thursdays: PT (e.g. 6:15) — protect it; keep the day from being overloaded.

## The model
- Fatima brain-dumps the week (Sun night); I generate **detailed, self-serve day sheets** for Ibrahim — time-blocked, each block opens to exact exercises/sets/reps/durations + a video per item. No planning visible to him, just "here's today."
